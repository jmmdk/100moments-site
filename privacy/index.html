Privacy Policy for 100 Moments

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the 100 Moments app ("the app") processes, why, and how you stay in control of it. In short: your data stays on your phone, an account is optional, and we show no ads and don't track you.

2. Information we process

Information that stays on your phone

When you use the app without an account, everything is stored locally on your phone:

  • About the children: name, age, a color and optionally a profile photo.
  • About the adventures: which ones you have completed and when, favorites and hidden adventures.
  • Photos you choose to add to a memory.
  • Settings: who you are (e.g. dad or mom), color theme and language.

We have no access to this information. It is deleted when you delete the app.

Account and backup (optional)

You can create an account with your email address (we send you a one-time code) or with Sign in with Apple. With an account, the app backs up the information above so you can restore it on a new phone:

  • Your email address (with Sign in with Apple, possibly an anonymous relay address from Apple).
  • The children's names, ages and colors, plus your adventures, favorites and hidden adventures.
  • Your settings (role, color theme, language) and the time of the latest backup.
  • With Premium: also photos of the children and photos in your memories.

Purchases and subscription

Purchases are made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to keep track of whether you have Premium. RevenueCat receives a random user ID (or your account ID if you are signed in) and information about your purchases from Apple/Google.

The children's names are only used to show text in the app, for example on the purchase screen. They are not sent to RevenueCat as part of your profile.

Location (optional)

If you allow it, the app uses your approximate location to find the name of your area (for example "Aarhus C"). Only the area name is kept, on your phone. When you ask for AI suggestions, the area name is used to look up nearby places (see below). We don't store it.

AI suggestions and pictures (optional)

When you use "Suggest with AI" or "Create with AI" (Premium), the app sends what is needed to make the suggestion or picture:

  • The area you search in is sent to Photon (komoot) to find real places nearby.
  • The category, the area, nearby places, the children's ages and the titles of your own adventures are sent to Anthropic (Claude) to write the suggestions. The children's names and photos are never sent.
  • A short description of the adventure is sent to Black Forest Labs (FLUX) to create a picture. The pictures contain no personal information and are stored with our hosting provider (Supabase) so the app can show them.

To share out the daily AI limit fairly, the app also sends a random ID that is not linked to your account or to you.

Usage statistics

To see how the app is used and where people get stuck (for example how far they get through the first setup), the app sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, hosted in the EU. An event is the name of a screen or action (for example "onboarding step viewed", "adventure completed" or "free trial started") with simple counts and flags, such as how many children or adventures. Events are tied to a random ID created by the app, not to your account, and we don't build profiles. The children's names, photos, your email address and your location are never sent.

Notifications

If you start a free trial, the app can remind you on day 5, two days before the trial ends. The reminder is scheduled locally on your phone. We don't use a server to send it.

Contacting us

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message in order to reply.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't track you across apps and websites.

4. Information about children

The app is meant for adults who use it together with their children. You, the adult, enter the information about the children. You decide how much to share: a nickname and no photos is fine. We don't use the children's information for marketing or profiling.

6. How long we keep information

  • Local data: until you delete it in the app or delete the app.
  • Account and backup: until you delete your account. You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and the whole backup, including photos.
  • Purchase records: as long as bookkeeping rules require (up to 5 years).
  • Messages to us: as long as needed to help you, at most 2 years.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the information we hold about you,
  • have incorrect information corrected,
  • have your information deleted,
  • restrict the processing,
  • receive your information in a common format (data portability),
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw your consent.

Write to hi@100moments.app to use your rights. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (www.datatilsynet.dk) or the data protection authority in your country.

8. Security

Information is sent encrypted (HTTPS). Backups and photos are only accessible to your own account. Photos in the backup are kept in private storage that only your account can access.

9. Changes

We update this policy if we change how we process information, for example when we add new features. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. For significant changes, we let you know in the app.
